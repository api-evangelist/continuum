# Continuum

> Continuum (gocontinuum.ai) is an AI-powered platform that automates B2B returns, warranties, and repairs for distributors and manufacturers. It manages the full post-sales workflow through four solution hubs — Customer, Warehouse, Vendor & Finance, and Manufacturer Warranty — and integrates with major distribution ERP systems. Based in Chicago and backed by Cowboy Ventures. SOC 2 audited. No public developer API is available at this time.

## Solutions
- [Customer Hub](https://gocontinuum.ai/customer-service-hub): Self-service return and warranty-claim portals.
- [Warehouse Hub](https://gocontinuum.ai/warehouse-hub): One-click receiving, inspection, and dispute processing.
- [Vendor & Finance Hub](https://gocontinuum.ai/vendor-finance-hub): Automated vendor engagement and accounts-receivable management.
- [Manufacturer Warranty Hub](https://gocontinuum.ai/manufacturer-warranty-hub): Warranty claim automation.
- [Integrations](https://gocontinuum.ai/integrations): Connectors for Infor, NetSuite, Epicor, Oracle, and Acumatica ERP systems.
